How much does a small business website cost in India?

22 October 2026 · 6 min read · By the Webs Design Studio team

₹

Website prices vary enormously. Some agencies quote lakhs, some freelancers quote a few thousand, and template builders charge monthly. The right price depends on what you actually need. Here is how to think about it.

What affects the cost

  • Number of pages. A one-page site is quicker than a site with six or more sections.
  • Custom design vs template. A design made around your business takes more care than a recycled template.
  • Features. A contact form is simple. Online ordering, bookings and blogs need more work.
  • Content. Writing and photos take time. Sites go live faster when you provide them.
  • Hosting and domain. These are yearly costs, separate from the design.

Watch out for these

  • Monthly fees for a site you can never take with you.
  • A domain registered in the agency's name instead of yours.
  • Extra charges for mobile-friendly design or basic search settings.
  • No way to make small edits after launch.

How to decide

Start with the simplest site that explains what you do and how to reach you. Add a blog or online ordering when you are ready. It is always easier to grow a good small site than to repair a bad big one.
