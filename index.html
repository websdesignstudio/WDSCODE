How long does it take?

Your website is ready in about 3 days after we receive your photos and text. A mini e-commerce site with payments takes around 5 days because we also set up your products and the payment gateway.

What do you need from me?

Your name and business, a clear photo or logo, your links and contact details, and a few samples of your work or products. If you have brand colours, send those too. If not, we will suggest some. Rough notes are fine, we do the writing.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, and you can take your site files to another host whenever you like. You are not renting a template.

Can I change things after it goes live?

Yes. Changes to text, links and photos are free for 30 days after launch. After that, small edits are quick and we tell you the price before we start.

Will people find my site on Google?

We set up the search basics: page titles, descriptions, fast loading, clean structure, structured data and a sitemap. Ranking also depends on your content and your competition, and a blog helps because it gives search engines more to find.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Most visitors open a site on a phone, so we design for small screens first and then scale up to tablets and laptops. You can test this on our sample sites by resizing the window or opening them on your phone.

Can customers order from my website?

Yes. We can add a cart so customers tap Add to cart, see a confirmation, and send the order to your WhatsApp. It suits shops, bakeries, cloud kitchens and boutiques. Try it on our Loom & Thread and Spice Route samples.

Can you build a website for a school, clinic or restaurant?

Yes. Our samples include a school, a hospital, a café, a cloud kitchen, a salon, an architecture studio, a jewellery store and more. Each one has separate pages such as home, services, admissions or menu, and contact.

What happens after the first year?

Hosting renews yearly at a small fee. You can cancel anytime and take your files with you.

Do you make logos or take photos?

No. We build and write the website. If you need a logo or photography we can point you to people we trust, and we will make your site look good with whatever you already have.

How do I pay?

After you send your order we reply with next steps and a payment link. You pay once for the design, and the first year of hosting is included.

Can I add a blog later?

Yes. You can start with the business site and upgrade to the blog plan any time. We will only charge the difference.
