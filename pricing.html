More answers in the FAQ on the home page.

How do I pay?

After you send your order we reply with next steps and a payment link. You pay once for the design, and the first year of hosting is included.

What happens after the first year?

Hosting renews yearly at a small fee. You can cancel anytime and take your files with you.

Can I change things after it goes live?

Yes. Changes to text, links and photos are free for 30 days after launch. After that, small edits are quick and we tell you the price before we start.

Can I add a blog later?

Yes. You can start with the business site and upgrade to the blog plan any time. We will only charge the difference.
